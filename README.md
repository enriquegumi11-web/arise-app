# ⚔️ ARISE

**Level up your real life.**

ARISE is an RPG-style life-management app. Every goal you complete gives you XP, and as you grow, you level up — just like in a game.

## 🎮 Core idea
- **XP system:** earn experience points for completing real-life goals and habits.
- **Levels:** watch your progress grow as you build discipline over time.

## 👥 Who it's for
Anyone who wants to make self-improvement feel fun, visual and rewarding.

## 🚧 Status
Early stage: concept and design. I'm learning Android development with Kotlin from the ground up and building ARISE step by step, in public.

## 🌱 Why open source
I'm a creative (actor, video editor, designer) learning to code. I want this repo to show that non-programmers can build their own tools, and to help other beginners — especially Spanish-speaking creatives — learn along with me.
